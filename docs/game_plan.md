## Tiny Gardener

A simple small game about you growing a garden. Game should be around 15 - 30 minutes. The goal is to make some money to buy something fancy.

You do grow some plants in your backyard trying to make money. The game does have two scenes. On is inside the house, here you can order some new seeds to grow or other gardening equipment if needed. 

The second scene is outside the house, where you can grow your garden.
